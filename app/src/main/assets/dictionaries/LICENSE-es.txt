Word frequencies: wordfreq 3.1.1
================================================================================

Word frequencies come from wordfreq (https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) by Robyn Speer. The wordfreq data is
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The frequencies in es.tsv are an adaptation of that data
(converted from Zipf values to integers) and are shared under the same license.

wordfreq's data is derived from Wikipedia, the OpenSubtitles corpus via ParaCrawl and OPUS, SUBTLEX, news and web
text, Google Books Ngrams, Reddit, Twitter and other sources; see the wordfreq README for the full list and credits.
The SUBTLEX data is freely available and was made by Marc Brysbaert, Boris New, Emmanuel Keuleers and others.
